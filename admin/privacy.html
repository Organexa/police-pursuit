Police Pursuit privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Police Pursuit is a mobile driving game by Organexa ("we") for players aged 13 and over. You can play it without an account. This page says what the game and the services inside it collect, and why.

If you play without signing in

  • Your coins, cars, best score and settings are stored on your phone.
  • We do not receive your name or email address.
  • The ads, purchase and notification services below still work as described.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

  • From Google: your email address, Google account ID and basic profile (name and picture link). We use them only to run your account.
  • From the game: your scores and run details (distance, time, near misses, car), your cloud save, when you last played, and a notification token.
  • Public: the leaderboard shows a player name (a generated one such as "Driver1234"), score, car and date to everyone. It does not show your email address or Google name.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with. Where those ads are enabled, they may collect your advertising ID and other device identifiers, your approximate location (worked out from your IP address), and crash and performance information, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

The game asks whether ad partners may use your data to show ads that fit you better. You can change your answer in Settings → Privacy & ads. If you say no, you still see ads.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details. The game only records what you unlocked.

Notifications

The game can send news and bonus messages using Firebase Cloud Messaging, which uses a device token. You can refuse or turn off notifications in your phone's settings.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase: accounts, scores and cloud save.
  • Google: sign-in, Google Play purchases and Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Unity and its advertising partners: ads.

We do not sell your account data. Data sent between the game and these services is encrypted (HTTPS). Their own privacy policies also apply.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep account data until you delete your account. You can do that in the game or by email: see how to delete your account. Data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game, though Android's own backup may keep a copy if you have it turned on.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.
