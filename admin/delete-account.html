Delete your Police Pursuit account

In the game

  1. Open Police Pursuit and sign in with Google.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Delete.

Without the game

Email rick.fbd@gmail.com from the Google account you used in the game, with the subject "Delete my Police Pursuit account". We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account, player name, leaderboard scores, cloud save and notification token. This cannot be undone.

Coins and cars stored on your phone stay until you uninstall the game. Purchases and advertising data are held by Google Play and the ad networks under their own privacy policies.
